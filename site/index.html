CS / 02

A visible motion budget for every site.

Generic reduced-motion preferences are easy for sites to ignore. Calm Scroll checks the page in front of you and shows what it found before you decide.

  1. 01

    Inspect the page

    See plain counts for autoplay media, animated elements, transforms, sticky layers, and smooth scrolling.

  2. 02

    Hold it steady

    One switch pauses autoplay and disables motion, including effects added after the page first loads.

  3. 03

    Keep useful controls

    Allow media or keep sticky navigation on the sites that need them. Each choice stays on this device.

Honest boundary

Comfort is personal. Control should not be.

Calm Scroll is not a medical device and does not promise to prevent nausea. It gives you direct, reversible control over common page motion. If a site breaks, turn stable mode off for that site or keep the controls it needs.
